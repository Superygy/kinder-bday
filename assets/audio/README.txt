AUDIO FILES

Optional site-wide background track:
- background-music.mp3

Featured favourite song:
- favourite-song.mp3

Dedicated final-letter soundtrack:
- final-letter-music.mp3
  This track starts with a soft crossfade as the final letter opens. It then
  continues through the remaining game and closing screens. It loops until
  the visitor leaves the experience.

The final-letter soundtrack path can be changed with `finalLetterMusic` in
../config.js. Both background tracks use the shared music toggle after the
final-letter transition. Browsers require a visitor interaction before
playback, so the finale track is primed by the successful vault submission.
